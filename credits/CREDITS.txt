Apill for VLC
=============

ORIGINAL SKIN
-------------
Apill, a skin for Winamp 3.
Copyright (c) 2001-2002 Brian Bryce (drwho9437). All artwork, layout and
color themes in this VLC skin are his work.

As credited in the original skin (skin.xml, version 0.92):
  Name:     Apill
  Comment:  "A simple, small, usable skin."
  Author:   Brian Bryce (drwho9437)
  Email:    brianbryce@bigfoot.com
  Homepage: http://wam.umd.edu/~bryceb/

The original skin was previously known as "Apple" and "Bluple" (see the
author's notes, included as Apill-original-notes.txt). In those notes, the
author thanks "Steve" for the equalizer script and the old-timers of the
Winamp 3 forum.

History, from the author's archived homepage:
  Bluple   v.92 by June 2002, a small blue skin for Winamp 3.
  Apple    v.20 in August 2002: Bluple with gammagroups (color themes) for
           Winamp 3 RC2. "It no longer has to be blue hence the name change."
  Apill    v.75 to v.92, August to October 2002: the same skin updated for
           Winamp 3 final, renamed again to avoid confusion with the
           computer company.
  http://web.archive.org/web/2002/http://wam.umd.edu/~bryceb/

Where to find the original Winamp 3 skin (Apill.wal):
  Winamp Heritage:  https://winampheritage.com/skin/apill/118660
  Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/winampskin_Apill

THIS PORT
---------
This is an unofficial port of Apill to VLC's skin engine (skins2). The
artwork is Brian Bryce's, recolored with the skin's own color presets and
rearranged for VLC. Nothing here is endorsed by the original author.
Brian Bryce: if you would like this port credited differently, changed or
taken down, please open an issue on the project page below.

VLC port by rspilk: https://github.com/rspilk/vlc-skin-rpill
The port's own code (build scripts and theme template) is MIT-licensed. That
license does not cover Brian Bryce's artwork or the fonts.
